How to play Tarneeb

Partners sit opposite each other. Aces are high. Play and bidding go counterclockwise: You → Right → Partner → Left.

1. Bid for your team

  • Each player receives 13 cards. Bid from 7 to 13: the number of tricks you think your team can win.
  • A bid must exceed the current bid. Passing removes you from the auction. If everyone passes, the same dealer redeals.
  • The highest bidder chooses trump and plays the first card. This version also allows no trump.

2. Play your cards

  • A trick is four cards, one from each player. Follow the suit of the first card if you have it.
  • If you have none of that suit, play any card. You are free to use trump or discard another suit.
  • The highest trump wins. Without trump, the highest card of the suit led wins. The winner leads next.
  • Cards with a gold edge are playable. “Suggest a move” gives you a reason for a recommended play.

First team to the selected target wins. There are regional scoring variants; these rules follow Pagat’s Tarneeb rules.

Your computer players

Cautious players bid conservatively and tend to preserve high cards. Aggressive players stretch bids and favor taking control. All computers follow the rules and use only their own cards and public information. Expert is a sampling strategy, so it can still make mistakes.

Playing with friends

Share your table invitation. Your browser remembers your seat so you can refresh and reconnect. After 30 seconds disconnected, a computer takes over until the next hand. New players enter between hands. Only the host can pause, start, or change seats.
